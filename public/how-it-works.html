Human-first boundaries

The judgment stays with your people.

We can help organize intake, follow-up, status visibility and drafts for review. We do not claim that software should decide every price, promise, diagnosis, exception or sensitive message on its own.

That is especially true in regulated or high-trust work. Healthcare, finance, childcare and similar categories need extra review before any live build is agreed.

FAQ

Questions owners ask before they are ready to enquire

That is normal. Bring one recent example where work got stuck. That is enough for discovery.

What if our current tools are messy?

That is part of the work. We check how the tools behave now instead of pretending every system is clean.

Do you support what gets built?

Yes, if you choose care. The support boundary is stated in writing instead of implied by the website.

Will this fit every industry on the examples page?

The examples show possible starting points, not a promise of fit for every business. We look at your tools, team and risks before agreeing what would work.
