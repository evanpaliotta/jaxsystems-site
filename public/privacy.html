Privacy

What the website collects, and what it does not.

How we handle the details you share when you contact Jax Systems.

When you use the enquiry form

We use your name, email, optional business name and message to understand your enquiry and reply to you. A hidden anti-spam field helps filter automated submissions. When enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the enquiry is likely from a person.

A delivered enquiry sends a notification to our Gmail inbox so we can follow up. Limited technical information may also be processed to deliver the form, prevent abuse and keep the site reliable. Cloudflare provides anti-spam protection and website hosting services.

What not to send

Do not send passwords, payment card details, medical records, social security numbers, or anything else more sensitive than needed for an initial business conversation.

Please share only what we need to understand the problem and contact you. We can discuss any more detailed information later, if it is needed for the work.

What happens to my enquiry after I send it?

Your enquiry stays in the inbox we use to respond and discuss possible work. Use the contact form if you want to ask us to correct or remove the details you shared. We do not automatically add enquiry contacts to a marketing list.

Are cookies required for the public site?

We do not use advertising trackers on this site. Cloudflare provides hosting and the Turnstile spam check, which may process device and network information to protect the form. Google provides the inbox currently used to receive enquiries.
