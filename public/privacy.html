Privacy

What the website collects, and what it does not.

This page covers the public site and enquiry form only. It does not invent broader promises about future client systems.

When you use the enquiry form

The frontend sends a JSON request to /api/contact with your name, email, optional business name, message, the hidden website honeypot, and an optional turnstile token if spam protection is enabled.

The site only reports success when the backend confirms actual delivery with {"ok": true, "id": "..."}.

What not to send

Do not send passwords, payment card details, medical records, social security numbers, or anything else more sensitive than needed for an initial business conversation.

If the free business mailbox is still being verified, the public contact path remains the form rather than a published mailbox address.

Does this page promise a specific retention schedule?

No. It stays accurate to the current public site: enquiry handling, delivery honesty and the kinds of information the form is built to avoid.

Are cookies required for the public site?

The site is built as static pages. Third-party spam protection is optional and only appears if the backend configuration provides a public Turnstile key.
