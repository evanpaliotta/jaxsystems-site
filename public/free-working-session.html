Free working session

A genuinely useful first session, with exact limits.

This is not a fake booking step. It is one working session on one question in one of seven business areas, up to 60 minutes, with the owner or decision-maker present. The goal is to make the next decision clearer, not to force a sale.

What you bring

One question, one part of the business, and the owner or decision-maker who can explain the current process and approve any safe change.

What happens in the session

Jax Systems reviews the current workflow with you in your own tools, measures only what the available records allow, and keeps the session inside the chosen question and area.

What you leave with

A short written summary covering what was reviewed, what was found or measured, one practical recommendation, and what still remains unknown.

Exact limits that stay exact.

These are commercial commitments, not placeholders.

The session boundaries

  • One session per business
  • Up to 60 minutes
  • One question in one area
  • Owner or decision-maker present
  • Broad small-business scope across the seven areas

Safety and consent

  • Use the client’s own tools
  • Read-only unless you approve a change
  • A small reversible fix may be made together when safe
  • No customer sends
  • No recording without consent

When the next step is clear.

If one improvement can be responsibly scoped, the next option is the direct standard build at $2,499. Paid build work still needs a signed scope card covering the trigger, end state, tools, rules, exclusions, tests, owner, and price.

When the next step is not clear.

That is when the optional $999 Business Improvement Plan exists. It reviews all seven areas at a high level, ranks opportunities, sets measurement, and gives one detailed implementation blueprint. If Jax Systems later builds the first standard improvement, the full $999 credits once toward the $2,499 total, leaving $1,500.
