Pricing and scope questions.

The numbers matter, but so do the rules around those numbers.

What if discovery shows the first problem is not a fit?

No standard deployment or care charge is introduced in that case. Discovery should still leave the owner with a clearer next step, and any broader work would require a separate quote before it starts.

What makes something a “standard first deployment”?

It is the first clearly scoped outcome that fits the agreed discovery path. Broader work such as migrations, deeper integrations, new locations or wider process changes is quoted separately before it starts.

When does the $249/month incremental care charge apply?

Only when an additional scoped outcome is both reusable and active. If that condition is not true, the incremental care charge does not apply.

What kinds of costs are separate from the listed prices?

Third-party fees are listed separately so you can distinguish Jax’s charges from outside tool or service costs used in the setup.

Discovery is meant to create clarity, not pressure.
