A Business Improvement Plan you can act on.

We look across the business to spot opportunities, then investigate the agreed priority areas in depth. The processes, people, inputs and timeframe we’ll cover are agreed before discovery begins—not an unlimited review of every part of the company.

  1. Current state

    A map of how the agreed processes work today, where work slows down, and what information is missing.

  2. Priorities

    A ranked set of opportunities to win more work, help customers or recover staff time. Not every business needs changes in all three.

  3. Recommendations

    What to simplify, connect, build or automate—and what should remain with people.

  4. Measurement

    Available baselines, proposed targets and a way to track progress. If a baseline is missing, the plan explains how to establish it. Estimates are identified; targets are not guaranteed results.

  5. First implementation

    The recommended starting point, responsibilities, what’s included, the checks for completion, and a fixed-price quote.

How we’ll know it helped.

Questions before you start.

What you’re agreeing to—and what you aren’t.

Does contacting Jax Systems start the $999 plan?

No. The introductory conversation is at no charge and is about your goals and whether we could help. It is not the paid analysis. We explain and agree the discovery work separately before it begins.

What if I only want the plan?

You keep the completed plan and walkthrough. There is no requirement to buy a build or monthly support.

What fits the $2,499 first standard build?

One agreed improvement whose tools, access, rules and completion checks are confirmed through discovery. More complex connections, moving data, new locations or wider changes need a separate quote. The price is confirmed before you decide to build.

Do I have to take monthly support?

No. You receive the agreed handoff and documentation as part of the build. Ongoing maintenance and adjustments are only included if we agree a support arrangement. Software provider fees may still apply.

What counts as a small adjustment?

A change to the same agreed solution, rather than adding a new capability or business process. We agree the allowance and the practical boundaries in your support terms before support begins.

When does the additional $249/month support apply?

Only for an extra improvement that can reuse the existing setup and approach and is live in your business. We agree what is covered first; a more complex project does not automatically qualify for this rate.

How long does discovery or a build take?

We agree the timeframe after confirming the work, the people involved and the access needed. There is no one-size-fits-all delivery promise.
