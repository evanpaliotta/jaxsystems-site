The free working session.

One of seven areas of your business, one specific question, up to 60 minutes. Your owner or decision-maker takes part.

What happens

We look together at one area, using your real examples and the client-owned tools you already have. You leave with a short written summary: what we looked at, what we found or measured, one practical recommendation and what remains unknown.

Small fixes, only when safe

If a small change is safe, reversible, within the area and agreed with you, we may make it during the session in your own tools. We don’t send messages to your customers for you during the session.

Clear limits

One free session per business. Access is read-only unless you approve a change. Anything beyond the session is quoted before work starts, and we record only with your consent.

Seven areas of every business.

Every business sells something, tells people about it, wins the work, delivers it, keeps customers, runs its operations and manages its money. We look at those areas, then go deeper only where you choose. We measure only what your records allow; the plan explains how to start where they don’t.

1. Offer

What you sell, to whom and how it’s priced. We’d measure: sales by service, average job value, where prices are set.

2. Marketing

How people find you and what they ask before they call. We’d measure: enquiries by source and the time to first reply.

3. Sales

How enquiries become quotes, bookings or signed work. We’d measure: quote turnaround, quotes awaiting a reply, enquiry-to-booking rate.

4. Delivery and customer experience

How work is fulfilled and customers are kept informed. We’d measure: time to first contact, missed updates, repeat problems.

5. Retention

How customers come back, refer others and leave reviews. We’d measure: repeat-customer rate, review requests sent, referrals.

6. Operations and people

Schedules, tools, information and handoffs between people. We’d measure: re-keyed entries, admin hours, handoff delays.

7. Finance

Invoicing, collecting payment and seeing costs clearly. We’d measure: days to invoice, days to collect, overdue balances.

What the Business Improvement Plan contains.

We agree the people, records and depth before we start. “All seven areas” means each area is considered at a practical level; deep work happens only where the plan recommends it and you agree.

  1. Business map

    How the seven areas work today, where work slows down and what information is missing.

  2. Priorities

    A ranked set of opportunities to win more work, help customers or recover staff time. Not every business needs changes in all three.

  3. Measurement

    Available baselines, proposed targets and how to track progress. If a baseline is missing, the plan explains how to collect one. Estimates are identified; targets are not guaranteed results.

  4. One implementation blueprint

    One detailed plan for your chosen improvement: the result, tools and accounts, step-by-step instructions, what your existing software already does, costs, tests, maintenance and what to do if it breaks.

  5. Fixed-price quote

    Only if you want Jax Systems to build it. Otherwise you can use the blueprint yourself or give it to another provider.

Questions before you start.

What you’re agreeing to—and what you aren’t.

Is the working session really free?

Yes. It covers one area, one specific question and up to 60 minutes, with one session per business. Anything beyond that is quoted before work starts.

Will I get a fix in the free session?

Sometimes. If a small change is safe and within the area you choose, we may make it together in your own tools. We don’t send messages to your customers for you.

Can I hire you without the $999 plan?

Yes. If we can confirm one improvement in the session, the standard build is $2,499. If the scope isn’t clear enough to price responsibly, we’ll recommend the plan instead.

What if I only want the plan?

You keep the completed plan, blueprint and walkthrough. There is no requirement to buy a build or monthly support.

Do I have to take monthly support?

No. You receive the agreed handoff and documentation as part of the build. Ongoing maintenance and adjustments are only included if we agree a support arrangement. Software provider fees may still apply.

What counts as a small adjustment?

A change to the same agreed solution, rather than adding a new capability or business process. We agree the allowance and the practical boundaries in your support terms before support begins.

When does the additional $250/month support apply?

Only for an extra improvement that can reuse the approved setup and access and is live in your business. We agree what is covered first; a more complex project does not automatically qualify for this rate.

How long does a session, plan or build take?

We agree the timing after confirming the work, the people involved and the access needed. There is no one-size-fits-all delivery promise.
