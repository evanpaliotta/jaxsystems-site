A few straight answers.

Do we need to replace our software?

Not usually the starting point. We inspect your current tools and permissions first. A native setting or a process improvement may be a better choice than adding a system.

What does “one deployment” mean?

One separately useful business result, with a named trigger, finished state, tools, rules, volume, exclusions and acceptance tests. It is not every individual technical step.

Why is a later deployment less expensive?

It can reuse connections and delivery patterns we already understand. But a later request can still be more complex; “from $999” is not a promise to build anything for that amount.

What happens if we don’t buy care?

You still receive the agreed system, documentation and handoff. Continued operation depends on the accounts, licenses and integrations involved. Ongoing responsibilities are made clear before work starts.

Can I book through this concept?

Not yet. The next-step page is a preview only, with no calendar, CRM or backend. Nothing is sent, and no appointment is booked.
